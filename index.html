How to use Yogprabha Facade Studio

Try colours and finishes on the Yogprabha exterior. The building's shape always stays exactly as drawn in the R5 elevations. Only colours and finishes change.

1 · Start from a combination

Click any of the 10 Recommended combinations on the left. It applies to all four sides and the 3D model at once.

2 · Change any part

  • Pick an element on the right (Main walls, Feature walls, Panels, Glazing, Frames & railings, Ground floor, Top), or just click that part on the drawing or the 3D model.
  • Choose a colour from the swatches or Custom colour, then a finish (paint, textured paint, concrete, brick, stone, wood, metal, tiles, glass). Pattern size scales bricks, stones and planks.
  • Gloss and Reflectivity control how shiny a surface looks in 3D. For glass, the colour is the tint.
  • If a part landed in the wrong element, click it and use Wrong element type? to move it.

3 · Views

  • All 4 sides / East / North / West / South show the flat elevations. Use − Fit + to zoom.
  • 3D view: drag to orbit, right-drag or Shift+drag to pan, scroll to zoom toward the cursor (right up to any balcony), W A S D to move, Q/E up/down. The corner buttons jump to set views.
  • Time slider: move the sun from morning to night. At night, the window lights come on.

4 · Save & share

Download image saves the flat elevations as a PNG. In 3D, use Win+Shift+S. Your last setup is remembered when you reopen the file. Reset goes back to the start.

Note: the R5 drawings give no projection depths, so the 3D facades are flat surfaces on the drawn lines. Nothing is added or moved.
